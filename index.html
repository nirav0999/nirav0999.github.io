Nirav Diwan

PhD @ UIUC

I am a 2nd year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Prof. Gang Wang in the Siebel School of Computing and Data Science. I study security risks in foundation model training and deployment.

Recently, I co-led the creation of PurpCode, which is the first reasoning model for cybersafety, winning the Amazon Nova AI Challenge (2025).

Internship. I am looking for both Industrial and Academic Internships for Summer 2026 in the areas of Machine Learning, and Security & Privacy. Feel free to reach out!

Collaboration. This is an open call for collaboration! Feel free to send me an email to talk about ideas, projects, collaborations, or research questions.

Industry experience

I interned at LG AI Research in summer 2024 with the Bi-lingual LLM team. I worked on alignment algorithms for LLMs with Tolga Ergen and Honglak Lee. Prior to this, I interned at Ema Unlimited in summer 2023 with the Generative AI Team on the problem of translating natural language intents to SQL Commands. I have also worked as a Natural Language Processing (NLP) Engineer at Prodigal Technologies for a year, where I focused on problems of Dialogue State Tracking (DST), Information Retrieval (IR), and optimizing search queries with a specific focus on finance-related conversations.

Prior Research Experience

Previously, I completed my research-track M.S. at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. Prior to this, I did my B.Tech. degree in Computer Science from IIIT Delhi in June 2021 where I was a member of LCS2. I worked on fingerprinting fine-tuned LLM-generated text with Prof. Zubair Shafiq (UC Davis) and Prof. Tanmoy Chakraborty (IIT Delhi). I also worked on RecipeDB, a large-scale recipe dataset, with Prof. Ganesh Bagler.
